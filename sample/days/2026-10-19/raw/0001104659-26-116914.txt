<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><B>Permianville Royalty Trust Announces Monthly

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">HOUSTON, Texas&mdash;(BUSINESS WIRE)&mdash;October&nbsp;19,
2026</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Permianville Royalty Trust (NYSE: PVL, the &ldquo;Trust&rdquo;)
today announced a cash distribution to the holders of its units of beneficial interest of $0.015000 per unit, payable on November&nbsp;13,
2026, to unitholders of record on October&nbsp;30, 2026. The net profits interest calculation represents reported oil production for the
month of July&nbsp;2026 and reported natural gas production during June&nbsp;2026. The calculation includes accrued costs incurred in
August&nbsp;2026.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">The following table displays reported underlying
oil and natural gas sales volumes and average received wellhead prices attributable to the current and prior month recorded net profits
interest calculations.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Recorded oil cash receipts from the oil and gas
properties underlying the Trust (the &ldquo;Underlying Properties&rdquo;) totaled $3.1 million for the current month on realized wellhead
prices of $81.06/Bbl, down $0.8 million from the prior month&rsquo;s oil cash receipts.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Recorded natural gas cash receipts from the Underlying
Properties totaled $2.4 million for the current month on realized wellhead prices of $3.05/Mcf, up $1.2 million from the prior month.
The increase in recorded natural gas production was due in part to the first month of associated production and revenues received from
three newly drilled wells in the Haynesville region.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Total accrued operating expenses decreased $0.3
million from the prior month to $2.6 million, and capital expenditures decreased $0.1 million from the prior month at $1.6 million.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">As previously disclosed, COERT Holdings 1 LLC
(the &ldquo;Sponsor&rdquo;) has established a cash reserve for approved, future development expenses associated with ongoing Haynesville
drilling, to account for the recent uptick in oil prices and the potential for increased capital expenditures by operators of the Underlying
Properties. Given the expected increase in spending, the Sponsor has notified the Trustee that it is withholding an additional $0.3 million
from the current month&rsquo;s net profits to be added to this cash reserve. To date, the Sponsor has established a total reserve of $2.0
million for approved, future development expenses. The Sponsor will hold this reserve to fund an expected increase in development expenses;
however, if those expenses are ultimately delayed or are less than expected, or if the outlook changes, amounts reserved but unspent will
be released as an incremental cash distribution in a future period.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Permianville Royalty Trust is a Delaware statutory
trust formed to own a net profits interest representing the right to receive 80% of the net profits from the sale of oil and natural gas
production from certain, predominantly non-operated, oil and gas properties in the states of Texas, Louisiana and New Mexico. As described
in the Trust&rsquo;s filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the &ldquo;SEC&rdquo;), the amount of the periodic distributions
is expected to fluctuate, depending on the proceeds received by the Trust as a result of actual production volumes, oil and gas prices,
the amount and timing of capital expenditures, and the Trust&rsquo;s administrative expenses, among other factors. Future distributions
are expected to be made on a monthly basis. For additional information on the Trust, please visit www.permianvilleroyaltytrust.com.</P>
