 <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman;font-weight:bold">Item&#8201;7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure. </p> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman;font-weight:bold">Portfolio and Business Commentary </p> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><span style="font-style:italic">(All figures as of September&#160;30, 2026, unless otherwise noted.) </span></p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">For the quarter ended September&#160;30, 2026, the Company&#8217;s net asset value (&#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">NAV</span>&#8221;) per share was $17.04, compared to $17.31 as of June&#160;30, 2026. The quarter-over-quarter change in NAV per share was primarily driven by distributions in excess of current-period net investment income, which were supported by accumulated distributable income from prior periods, and net unrealized depreciation from certain of the Company&#8217;s investments in the portfolio. Net investment income was $0.44 per share for the quarter ended September&#160;30, 2026. Dividends for the quarter ended September&#160;30, 2026 were $0.53 per share, representing an annualized distribution yield of 12.44% based on third quarter NAV per share.<sup style="font-size:75%; vertical-align:top">1</sup> The Company&#8217;s quarterly and <span style="white-space:nowrap"><span style="white-space:nowrap">year-to-date</span></span> return through September&#160;30, 2026 were 1.5% and <span style="white-space:nowrap">-11.0%,</span> respectively. For the quarter ended September&#160;30, 2026, approximately 64% of the gross unrealized losses were driven by two portfolio companies, comprised of one Level&#160;3 asset and one Level&#160;2 asset (all Level&#160;3 assets are valued by an independent third party). </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">As of September&#160;30, 2026, the fair market value of the Company&#8217;s portfolio was $261.6&#160;million across 43 portfolio companies and 14 industries. The Company&#8217;s unfunded commitments were $12.9&#160;million at September&#160;30, 2026. The Company&#8217;s portfolio was positioned with 97% first lien debt investments and 99% floating rate investment commitments based on par value, underscoring the Company&#8217;s focus to position the portfolio at the top of the capital structure.
